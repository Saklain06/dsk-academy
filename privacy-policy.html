Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

At DSK Academy (operated via dskacademy.co.in), we prioritize the privacy and security of our learners, visitors, and partners. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal data we collect, how it is stored and utilized, and your rights concerning your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our platform, register for courses, or submit inquiries, we may collect:

  • Contact Data: Your name, email address, telephone number, and communication preferences.
  • Account Credentials: Login credentials, username, and encrypted passwords.
  • Academic Progress: Enrolled courses, assignment submissions, attendance records, and certification progress.
  • Payment Verification: Transaction reference IDs and order receipts processed securely via licensed payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay/Stripe). We never store credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process student information solely for legitimate educational and administrative purposes:

  • Delivering courses, live webinars, mentoring sessions, and curriculum materials.
  • Generating verifiable DSK Academy course completion certificates.
  • Responding to admission inquiries, customer support queries, and technical assistance requests.
  • Sending critical academic announcements, syllabus updates, and scheduling notifications.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption), secure cloud hosting infrastructure, and restricted administrative access to safeguard your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Third-Party Sharing

DSK Academy does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is shared strictly with essential service providers necessary for operating the academy (such as authorized payment gateways, transactional email providers, and webinar tools like Zoom/Google Meet).

6. Cookies & Analytics

We use functional and analytical cookies (such as Google Analytics) to understand website performance and improve the student user experience. You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time.
