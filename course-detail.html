3D Modeling with Blender

Master the fundamentals of 3D design using Blender, the world’s leading free and open-source 3D software. This course is designed for beginners and aspiring creators who want to learn how to build 3D models, add textures, and render stunning scenes from scratch. Through hands-on projects and step-by-step instruction, you'll gain real-world skills to bring your creative ideas to life whether you're aiming for games, animation, or digital art.

1. What You’ll Learn
  • Navigating Blender's interface and shortcuts
  • Creating 3D objects using basic and advanced modeling tools
  • Applying materials, shaders, and realistic textures
2. Tools Used
  • Blender (latest stable version)
  • Optional: Photoshop or GIMP for texture editing
  • Optional: Sketchfab for model preview
3. Who This Course Is For
  • Beginners interested in 3D design and animation
  • Game designers, illustrators, or digital artists expanding their skills
  • Anyone looking to enter the world of 3D creation with zero cost
